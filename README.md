# UFC Fight Predictor

A UFC fight card prediction game. Before each event, lock in your picks — who wins, and how the fight ends — then get scored once the card actually plays out.

## Why this exists

A personal project born out of 7 years of Muay Thai training and genuine UFC fandom, not a generic app idea. UFC runs events almost every weekend, so unlike a one-off novelty project, this is something there's a real, recurring reason to open.

## What it does (concept level)

- Browse upcoming UFC events and fight cards, pulled from real fight data rather than manually maintained lists.
- Lock in a prediction per fight — who wins, and optionally how (decision, KO/TKO, submission) and even which round.
- Once a card airs, predictions are automatically scored against the real result. More specific, harder predictions are worth more.
- A running leaderboard tracks accuracy across an entire year, not just a single event, so a group of friends can compete over a whole season.
- A later "Discover Fighters" feature — recommends fighters with a similar style or stat profile to ones you already follow or have predicted on.
